The challenge

Turn one scenario into an original AI video.

On competition day the organizers open the gate. Each accepted team starts its own 20-minute timer, generates a video from the brief, and submits a Google Drive link with the video and prompts.

Video and prompt requirements

  • Follow the provided story, scene instructions and required visual elements.
  • Use clear, structured prompts and refine them for motion and consistency.
  • Create the actual video during your timed window; previously completed videos cannot be submitted.
  • Submit a Google Drive link that contains the video and the prompts you used.
  • Editing, audio and subtitles are allowed, but AI generation must form the core video content.

Fairness and conduct

Do not copy another team's prompts or work, fabricate generation evidence, or include unauthorized or harmful content. Judges may inspect prompts and verify the work; their evaluation decision is final.
